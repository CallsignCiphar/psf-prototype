About PSF

Independent analysis of the forces shaping Pakistan's security and the region around it.

Who we are

Pakistan Strategic Forum is a defence and military think tank. We study how wars are fought, how states prepare for them and how they are prevented. Our work covers the armed forces and defence industry, national security, regional geopolitics and the military history that explains the present.

We publish for decision-makers, scholars, journalists and readers who want evidence rather than noise. Every piece we publish is labelled for what it is: reporting, analysis, opinion or peer-reviewed research.

What we do

Analysis

Timely, sourced analysis of defence and security developments, written by analysts, practitioners and scholars.

Research

Peer-reviewed papers and policy briefs through PSF Research, open to scholars across the region.

Newsroom

Fast, verified updates on events that matter to Pakistan's security environment.

How we work

We separate reporting from opinion. We correct errors openly and log every correction. Research is reviewed double-blind by independent reviewers before publication.
