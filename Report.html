Annual Outlook 2027: the South Asia security environment

Deterrence remains stable but more brittle, the western border is the most active front, and maritime competition is rising faster than budgets.

PSF Analysis Desk14 Oct 2026, 10:00 PKT · 22 min read
Download PDF

Executive summary

2027 opens with a deterrence relationship that has held through two crises but now rests on shorter warning times. Pressure on the western border will rise as militant groups consolidate. At sea, regional navies are expanding faster than their budgets can sustain.

Key findings

  1. 1Deterrence is stable but brittle: decision windows are shorter than at any point since 1998.
  2. 2The western border is now the most active front, measured by incidents per month.
  3. 3Naval expansion across the region is outpacing sustainment budgets.

1. The deterrence picture

Two crises in five years have tested deterrence between India and Pakistan. Both ended without escalation, but the margin was narrower each time.
