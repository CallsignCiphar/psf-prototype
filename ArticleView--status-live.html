Water security and the Indus Waters Treaty

Treaty mechanisms have held through three wars. Whether they hold through a hotter, drier basin is a different question.

SHSara HaiderMPhil candidate, Quaid-i-Azam University
Cover image

The Indus Waters Treaty has outlasted three wars and decades of hostile rhetoric. Its dispute mechanisms, the Permanent Indus Commission, the neutral expert and the court of arbitration, have each been used, and each has produced outcomes both sides could live with.

The pressure now is hydrological rather than political. Glacier melt is front-loading flows, monsoon variability is widening, and the treaty assumes a river that is changing beneath it.
