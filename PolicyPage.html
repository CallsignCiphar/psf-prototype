Editorial Policy

Last updated 16 Oct 2026

1. Purpose

This policy sets out the standards Pakistan Strategic Forum applies to everything it publishes.

2. Independence

PSF's editors make all editorial decisions. No advertiser, partner, contributor or funder may approve, alter or suppress content. Sponsored or partner content, if ever published, carries a clear "Sponsored" or "In partnership with" label.

3. Accuracy and sourcing

We verify facts before publication. We attribute information to its source wherever possible. When we rely on anonymous sources, an editor must know the source's identity and judge them credible, and we explain why anonymity was granted.

4. Labelling

Every item carries a label: News, Analysis, Explainer, Opinion, Interview, Investigation, Profile, Book Review, Report, Research Paper or Policy Brief. Opinion pieces reflect the views of their authors, not of PSF.

5. Sensitive security information

We do not publish information that would endanger lives, identify protected individuals or compromise ongoing security operations.

Questions about this policy? Contact PSF.
